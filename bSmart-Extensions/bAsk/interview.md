# bAsk interview

Use this to sharpen any idea, plan, or decision by asking the human
questions. Standalone, or for a bMap explore ticket.

## Rules

1. **One question at a time.** Wait for the answer before the next one.
2. **Short.** A question is one or two plain sentences. Add up to four
   options when that helps, and your recommended answer. Never three
   paragraphs.
3. **Word it so "yes" accepts your recommendation.** For example: "Store the
   maps per project? (Recommended: yes, it matches the backlog rule.)"
4. **The human decides.** Never answer your own question. Never pick for the
   human. If they say "you choose", give your recommendation and ask them to
   confirm it.
5. **Look facts up yourself.** Do not ask for anything you can find in files,
   docs, or tools. Send a low-effort helper for it and keep asking the
   questions that do not depend on that fact.
6. **Ask in order.** Ask first what other questions depend on. Each answer can
   open or close later questions.
7. **Use the project's words.** If the human uses a term that clashes with the
   project's knowledge or glossary, say so in one line and ask which is meant.
8. **Plain language**, in the human's own language. Explain jargon in a few
   words.
9. **Show the way.** Now and then say roughly how many questions are left.
10. **Stop** when nothing important is left open, or when the human says
    stop. Do not ask questions only to fill a list.

## Finish

1. Sum up the decisions as a short list, one line each, and anything still
   open.
2. Ask the human to confirm. Do not act on the result before they do.
3. Record it where it belongs:
   - standalone: in the chat, or in the project's `decisions.md` if the human
     wants it kept;
   - bMap explore ticket: in the ticket's `## Answer`, with `decided_by`
     set to the human.
4. If a build brief is needed, offer bQAbuild. If the idea turns out too big
   for one session, offer bMap.

Asking questions never gives permission to edit, build, commit, or publish.
